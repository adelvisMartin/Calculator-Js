# Support

InSave is currently a public **QA / Recovery** project, not a commercial support
service and not yet a production-stable Play Store release.

## Where to ask for help

Use GitHub issues for reproducible, non-sensitive problems involving:

- app crashes or startup failures;
- download/provider regressions;
- WhatsApp/WhatsApp Business status discovery;
- UI/navigation defects;
- build/reconstruction failures;
- documentation mistakes.

Do not post secrets or private account/session material. Security-sensitive
reports must follow `SECURITY.md`.

## Before opening an issue

Include:

- exact InSave version and APK filename;
- Android version and device model;
- whether the app was installed cleanly or updated over an older build;
- exact steps to reproduce;
- expected vs actual result;
- sanitized logs/screenshots when useful;
- whether the problem reproduces with a public test URL.

## Unsupported requests

The project does not promise support for:

- bypassing access controls, DRM or paywalls;
- private content for which the user lacks authorization;
- stolen/third-party credentials or cookies;
- arbitrary modified APKs distributed by third parties under the InSave name;
- forks that retain InSave branding in violation of `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## Release authenticity

For official QA builds, verify the published filename/version and SHA-256 before
installing. A renamed APK is not a new InSave release.
